ForgetMeNot

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

Overview

ForgetMeNot is developed and provided by ForgetMeNot Studio.

This Privacy Policy explains how ForgetMeNot accesses and handles information when you use the application.

1. Information ForgetMeNot accesses

ForgetMeNot may access information about the Bluetooth device that you choose to protect, including:

  • Bluetooth device name
  • Bluetooth device address
  • Bluetooth connection status

This information is used only to provide the app's Bluetooth protection and disconnect-alert functionality.

ForgetMeNot may also store:

  • Your selected protected device
  • Whether Protection is enabled
  • Your selected alert delay
  • Your selected alert sound preference
  • Recent protection activity and alert history
  • Onboarding and application preference settings

This information is stored locally on your device.

2. Data collection and sharing

ForgetMeNot does not operate its own backend servers and does not transmit the information described above to ForgetMeNot Studio.

The current version of ForgetMeNot does not:

  • Sell personal information
  • Share personal information with advertisers
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Use analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs
  • Require a user account
  • Collect precise or approximate location
  • Upload Bluetooth device information to the developer
Information processed locally on your device is used only to provide the application's functionality.

3. Bluetooth access

ForgetMeNot requests Bluetooth permission so that it can identify the Bluetooth device selected by the user and monitor that device's connection state.

Bluetooth access is required for the core Protection feature.

ForgetMeNot does not use Bluetooth information for advertising, profiling, or tracking users across apps or services.

4. Notifications and foreground protection

ForgetMeNot may request permission to send notifications.

Notifications are used to:

  • Show that Protection is currently active
  • Display the connection status of the protected device
  • Warn you when the protected device remains disconnected beyond your selected alert delay

While Protection is enabled, ForgetMeNot uses an Android connected-device foreground service so that Bluetooth protection can continue while the application is in the background.

An ongoing system notification is displayed while this service is active.

You can stop Protection at any time from within the application or from the ongoing notification.

5. Device restart

If you previously enabled Protection, ForgetMeNot may restore that protection after your Android device restarts or after the application is updated.

ForgetMeNot checks whether Protection was previously enabled before restoring monitoring.

If Protection was not enabled, the background protection service is not automatically started.

6. Local storage and backups

ForgetMeNot stores application preferences and protection information in private application storage on your Android device.

ForgetMeNot is configured not to include its private application data in Android cloud backup or automatic device-to-device backup.

Uninstalling the application normally removes locally stored ForgetMeNot application data from the device.

7. Protection history and deletion

ForgetMeNot stores a limited history of recent protection events locally on your device.

Because ForgetMeNot does not maintain user accounts or developer-controlled cloud storage, there is no remote account or server-side profile to delete.

You can remove locally stored application data by:

  • Using any available history-clearing option within the application, where applicable
  • Clearing ForgetMeNot's storage through Android system settings
  • Uninstalling ForgetMeNot

8. Third-party software

ForgetMeNot uses Android and AndroidX libraries as well as Google Play services components required for application functionality and open-source license notices.

The Google Play services OSS Licenses SDK used by ForgetMeNot does not collect end-user data.

Open-source license information can be viewed from within the application.

9. Security

ForgetMeNot is designed to minimize data handling by processing protected-device information locally on the user's device.

No internet-based ForgetMeNot account or developer-operated server is used to store protected-device information in the current version of the application.

No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free, but ForgetMeNot limits exposure by avoiding unnecessary transmission of user information.

10. Children's privacy

ForgetMeNot is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

The application does not require an account and does not operate a developer-controlled service for collecting user information.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ForgetMeNot's features or data practices change.

For example, if a future version introduces advertising, analytics, cloud services, accounts, or other functionality that changes how user information is handled, this Privacy Policy and the application's Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be updated before or when those changes are released.

The effective date at the top of this Privacy Policy will indicate when it was last updated.
